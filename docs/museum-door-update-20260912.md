# Museum doors and room capacity — 2026-09-12

The 100 heritage exhibits now occupy five themed rooms with 20 distinct exhibits each: 火土新生、经纬成章、竹木清韵、金漆流光、灯影雅集. Rooms are 12 units wide and 25 units deep, with ten paired display rows and an extended viewing rail.

All corridor entrances share a real Tripo GLB door with carved red wood, bronze studs and a lion knocker. Three.js loads, fits and animates the asset; the invisible plane only supports picking. The Tripo model faces +X and is rotated before fitting to the doorway.
